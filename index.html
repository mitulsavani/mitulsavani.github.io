I lead forward-deployed engineering at HuLoop Automation. That means designing secure environments that bank security teams sign off on, planning capacity for attended and unattended digital workers, deciding where humans stay in the loop, and staying accountable for whether the system actually runs. What we learn in the field goes back to our product team as platform improvements.

I manage and mentor a team of more than 12 engineers, and I've built it as much as led it: I own hiring, growth, and performance decisions. My engineers join discovery sessions, solution design, client conversations, and production deployments, so each of them can own a customer use case end to end.

Before HuLoop, I spent five years architecting and shipping enterprise mobile apps for government agencies, and contributed to Meta's React Native. I came to San Francisco from Surat, India, at 18 to study computer science and mathematics, and never stopped building.

Away from work, you'll find me on the golf course, training for Hyrox and CrossFit, or spending quality time with my family.
